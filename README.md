# EU3DT3DS
The 3DS version of Terraria has no 3D capability, So someone made a mod that you put on your SD card and it gives you that 3D capability back. Issue is that mod is for the USA version of Terraria only, This one is for the EU & AU (PAL) version. This ONLY works on the _N3DS/N3DSXL/N2DSXL_, It will NOT work on the _O3DS/O3DSXL/O2DS_. I did not convert it or build it myself, Claude did. The user who made the USA version is: [mattvideoproductions](https://github.com/mattvideoproductions).

You can download the EU & AU (PAL) 3D Terraria Mod [Here](https://github.com/Astro-15/eu3dt3ds/releases/download/terraria/Terraria3D_1.3gx), And the USA 3D Terraria Mod [Here](https://github.com/mattvideoproductions/Terraria3D)

Check out my Video on the EU & AU (PAL) 3D mod [Here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iMpRLb_fogo).

PS: I named it `EU3DT3DS` as i thought my copy of Terraria was the EU version only, It's not, It is the PAL version. I will not be changing it to be `PAL3DT3DS` as imo that sounds stupid. `EU3DT3DS` is funnier to say.
